## 5. Voice & tone

**Voice = first person, confident, professional.** Clear and specific, not slogan-y. The visuals are LOUD; the words are grown-up and credible. State what is real, plainly.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "I produce video, photography and branding, end to end." | "I don't document. I direct." (corny tagline) |
| "Eight years across events, brands and campaigns." | "Every frame earns its place." (empty slogan) |
| "Concept to final grade, one vision throughout." | "I don't do boring." / "Zero filler." (try-hard) |
| Specific, grounded claims | Rhyming or "I don't X, I Y" constructions |
| Speak as **I** (personal) | Speak as "we/our team" (no fake team) |
| Confident and professional | Hype, exclamation spam, ad-copy clichés |

**Hard copy rules:**
- **First person ("I"), always.** Never "we."
- **No em dashes (—) ever.** Use commas, periods, or restructure. (Standing brand rule.)
- **Bilingual EN + FR.** Every caption ships in both. French is Québec-natural, not translated-stiff.
- No emojis as bullet icons in designed pieces. Sparingly in captions only if on-brand.

## 6. Visual direction — TWO MODES

Both modes share: **film grain always**, **red #E8181C as accent only** (never large fills), **sharp corners**, high contrast, cinematic.

### Mode A — LOUD (A*VENUE energy)
The statement/brand/manifesto look.
- Massive condensed grotesque type (Barlow Condensed weight), set tight, often overlapping
- Palette: red `#E8181C` + near-black `#0a0a0a` + bone `#f0ede8`
- Heavy grain, halftone, photocopy/risograph texture, poster + editorial collage
- B&W or high-contrast portraits with red type laid over
- Accents: asterisk mark, graffiti scrawl, registration marks, address/contact set as micro-type
- Energy: bold, confrontational, "join the movement," screenprint/poster

### Mode B — ATMOSPHERIC (cinematic)
The mood/emotional/filler look.
- Cinematic stills, motion blur, heat-map color (orange/red glow vs teal/cold), deep shadow
- Sparse Space Mono micro-type, lots of negative space
- Quiet, emotional, film-still energy ("WHAT A PRIVILEGE," "WHEN WE ALL FALL ASLEEP" poster vibe)
- Subject often a lone figure, silhouette, or detail

**When to use which:** LOUD = brand statements, services, manifestos, launch. ATMOSPHERIC = mood pieces, BTS, transitions, captions that breathe, "fillers" that keep the grid cinematic.
